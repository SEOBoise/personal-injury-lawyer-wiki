Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Personal Injury Lawyer Wiki handles information when people visit this independent, static legal-reference website.

Information visitors provide

The site does not provide user accounts, comments, purchases, newsletter registration or a direct-contact form. It therefore does not ask visitors to submit names, email addresses, mailing addresses, payment information or case details through the website.

Local page search

The search field on the article page searches headings and text already loaded in the visitor's browser. The query is not submitted to a Personal Injury Lawyer Wiki database or retained by the site as a search record.

Technical and security data

Hosting, content-delivery and security infrastructure may automatically process routine request data such as an Internet Protocol address, requested URL, browser or device information, referring page, timestamps and security events. This information may be used to deliver pages, maintain reliability, detect abuse and protect the site.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

Personal Injury Lawyer Wiki does not currently configure first-party analytics, advertising, remarketing or social-media tracking pixels. The site's own pages do not require an account or preference cookie. Hosting and security providers may use necessary technical mechanisms to route requests, prevent abuse or maintain service integrity.

External websites

The article identifies selected law-firm websites, and the source directory displays addresses for government, legal, insurance and research resources. External sites operate under their own privacy practices. Personal Injury Lawyer Wiki does not control their content, data collection or security.

Data retention and security

Because the site does not collect information through accounts or forms, it does not maintain a visitor-submission database. Infrastructure providers may retain technical logs under their own operational and security practices. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Children's privacy

This general-reference site is intended for a broad audience and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly request personal information from children.

Policy changes

This policy may be revised when the site's features, providers or practices change. A revised version will state its effective date on this page.
